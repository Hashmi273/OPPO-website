Who we are

A legacy built on integrity, engineered for tomorrow.

Shapoorji Pallonji Group is one of India's leading conglomerates, with diversified businesses in Engineering & Construction, Infrastructure, Energy, Real Estate, Water and Financial Services.

We operate across 6 major business areas with 13 group companies and 3 divisions, powered by a workforce of over 60,000 people from 40+ nationalities.
